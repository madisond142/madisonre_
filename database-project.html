Lifecycle

Process

Our consulting team structured the database development lifecycle across five iterative stages:

1

Business Rules

Conducted stakeholder interviews with [client name] to capture and document precise business rules governing customers, orders, inventory, transactions, and employee roles.

2

ER Diagram

Drafted conceptual Entity-Relationship diagrams in ERDPlus, identifying entity types, primary keys, and cardinality constraints (1:1, 1:N, M:N) while resolving many-to-many associations.

3

Relational Schema

Mapped the ER diagram into a normalized relational schema. Ensured Third Normal Form (3NF) compliance to protect data integrity, prevent anomalies, and establish foreign key relationships.

4

MySQL Tables and Data

Wrote DDL scripts in MySQL Workbench to create physical database tables with constraints (PRIMARY KEY, FOREIGN KEY, NOT NULL, UNIQUE) and populated them with realistic sample records.

5

Business Questions Answered with SQL

Formulated and validated 10 real-world business queries utilizing INNER/LEFT JOINs, subqueries, aggregate functions (COUNT, SUM, AVG), and GROUP BY/HAVING filtering to provide managerial insights.
